Nothing is kept. Google tells this page your account's ID and email after you sign in. The page only shows them: it doesn't save or send them anywhere, and closing it forgets them. Signing in here doesn't add the account to any phone.

How it works

  1. Tap Sign in with Google and pick the account (one the child doesn't know the password to).
  2. Copy the ID.
  3. On the child's phone: LazerLock → Restrictions → Protect LazerLock → Google accounts. Type the email and the ID.
